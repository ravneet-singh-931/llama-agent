Kestrel Outdoor Co. - Board Report, Third Quarter 2026

1. Summary

Kestrel Outdoor Co. designs and sells camping, hiking and climbing equipment through its own website, its retail partners and three flagship stores. This report covers the quarter from July 1 to September 30, 2026, and was prepared for the board meeting on October 15, 2026.

Revenue for the quarter was $48.6 million, an increase of 12% compared with the same quarter last year. Gross margin was 41.2%, up from 39.8%, mainly because fewer products were sold at a discount. Online sales made up 37% of revenue. Net income was $4.9 million.

2. Revenue by region

North America remained the largest market with revenue of $29.1 million. Europe contributed $13.4 million, helped by a strong summer season in Germany and the Nordics. Asia-Pacific contributed $6.1 million; growth there was slower than planned because the launch in Japan was moved to next year.

3. E-commerce platform

The migration of the online store to Shopify was completed in August 2026, two weeks ahead of schedule and within budget. Since the switch, the website conversion rate has improved from 2.1% to 2.6%, and the average page load time has fallen by about a third. The old platform contract ends in December, which will save roughly $400,000 a year in licence fees.

4. Capital allocation

The board approved a capital allocation plan of $7.5 million for the second half of the year. It has four parts. First, $3.0 million goes to a new distribution centre in Reno, Nevada, which will shorten delivery times to customers on the west coast. Second, $2.0 million is set aside for a share buyback programme. Third, $1.5 million funds research and development for a new line of lightweight tents. Fourth, $1.0 million will be used to repay part of the revolving credit facility early. No dividend will be paid this year.

5. Balance sheet and operations

Cash and cash equivalents stood at $21.3 million at the end of the quarter. Inventory turnover was 4.8 times on an annualised basis. The company employed 412 people at quarter end, including seasonal staff in the stores.

The returns rate for online orders was 6.4%, slightly better than last year. Marketing spend was 9% of revenue, in line with the plan. The customer Net Promoter Score rose to 54 after the new free-repair service was introduced in July.

6. Leadership

Priya Raman joined the company as Chief Financial Officer on July 1, 2026. She was previously the finance director of a European sporting goods retailer. The search for a new head of supply chain is ongoing.

7. Risks

The main risks for the coming quarters are: new tariffs on imported aluminium, which is used in tent poles and climbing hardware; a weaker euro, which reduces the dollar value of European sales; and the dependence on two factories in Vietnam for most backpacks. To reduce the factory risk, a third supplier in Portugal is being qualified and should start production in the second quarter of 2027.

8. Outlook

For the fourth quarter of 2026, management expects revenue between $55 million and $58 million, reflecting the holiday season. Gross margin is expected to stay above 40%.
